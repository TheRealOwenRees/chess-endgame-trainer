Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Inner Square Chess is an endgame training site. This policy explains what information we collect when you use it, why we collect it, and the choices you have. It covers the website and every page under it, including the guest trainer.

What we collect

  • Account details. If you register, we store your username, email address, and a hashed password. We never store passwords in plain text.
  • Training activity. We record the puzzles you attempt and whether you solved them, so we can show your progress and keep the daily puzzle meaningful.
  • Mailing list email. If you join the mailing list — using the form on the home page or the checkbox when registering — we store your email address and where you signed up, so we can send you occasional updates. You can ask to be removed at any time.
  • Technical data. Our hosting and server infrastructure may keep standard request logs (IP address, browser, timestamps) for security and troubleshooting. These logs are rotated regularly.
  • Messages you send us. If you contact us, we keep the message and our reply for as long as needed to help you.

Cookies and local storage

We use a small number of cookies and local storage entries that are required for the site to work:

  • a session cookie that keeps you signed in;
  • a CSRF token that protects forms from cross-site requests;
  • a colour-mode preference stored in your browser.

We do not use advertising cookies, third-party analytics, or cross-site tracking. You can clear these entries at any time in your browser settings; doing so will sign you out and reset your colour mode.

How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To run the trainer and remember your progress.
  • To send service emails, such as confirmation and password reset links.
  • To send occasional endgame tips and site news, if you opted in.
  • To keep the site available, prevent abuse, and fix faults.
  • To respond when you ask for help.

We rely on our contract with you to provide the service, our legitimate interests in keeping it secure and improving it, and your consent where we ask for it. You can withdraw consent at any time.

Guest puzzle limit

Visitors who use the guest trainer without an account get a limited number of free puzzles per UTC day. To enforce that limit and prevent abuse, we identify each visitor by a keyed hash (HMAC-SHA256) of their IP address. The limiter itself never stores, logs, or shares the raw address.

The hash exists only in the memory of the server that handled your request. It is tied to the current UTC day, stops being usable at 00:00 UTC, and is deleted by a periodic sweep shortly afterwards; it is also discarded when the server restarts. It is never written to a database, a log, or a backup, and no cookie or other client-side storage is involved. Signed-in accounts are not metered this way.

We process this hash on the basis of our legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) in keeping the service available and preventing abuse of the free tier. We do not share it with anyone. You have the right to object to this processing; to do so, contact us using the address below.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with providers who help us run the site — for example hosting, database, and email delivery providers — and only as far as they need it to perform that work. We may also disclose information where the law requires it or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

Retention

We keep account and training data while your account is open. If you delete your account, we remove your personal information within a reasonable period, except where we must keep records for legal, security, or accounting reasons.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or export your personal information, and to object to some uses of it. To make a request, contact us using the address below. If you are in the UK or EU and are unhappy with our response, you can complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

The service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes

We may update this policy as the service changes. When we do, we will change the date at the top of this page and, for significant changes, notify signed-in users by email or on the site.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to support@innersquare.app.
